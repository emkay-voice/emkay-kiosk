# Emkay on an iPhone or iPad

An iPad on the wall or an old iPhone on a stand makes a lovely Emkay. iOS has everything needed
built in, so there is nothing to install apart from Emkay itself.

> **Can it run Emkay?** Emkay does speech on the device by default, and that needs a recent Safari.
> On older iPhones and iPads, Emkay says so on its first start. You can then let your
> [Emkay Home](https://github.com/emkay-voice/emkay-home) do the speech, or choose OpenVoiceOS'
> servers (Emkay tells you what that means for privacy).

## 1. Open Emkay and add it to the Home Screen

1. Open **Safari** and go to <https://app.emkay.ai> (or your Emkay Home's address).
2. Tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open Emkay from its new icon on the Home Screen. It now runs on its own, without Safari's address
   bar, and keeps its settings and downloaded models apart from Safari.
4. Let Emkay finish its first start and allow the **microphone** when asked.

## 2. Keep the screen on

- Say *"Hey Emkay, keep the screen on"*, or open the ⚙ menu → control panel → *Screen*.
- As a fallback, set **Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → Never**.

## 3. Lock the device to Emkay with Guided Access

Guided Access keeps the iPhone or iPad in one app. The Home button, swipes and notifications are
switched off until you end it with your passcode.

1. Go to **Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access** and turn it on.
2. Under **Passcode Settings**, set a Guided Access passcode (or allow Face ID / Touch ID to end it).
3. Under **Display Auto-Lock**, choose **Never**, so the screen stays on while Guided Access runs.
4. Open Emkay, then **triple-click the side button** (or the Home button on older models) and tap
   **Start**.

To end it: triple-click again and enter the passcode.

## 4. Power and battery

- Keep the device on its charger.
- On iPhones that have it, go to **Settings → Battery → Charging** and choose a limit such as
  **80 %**. This is easier on a battery that is charging all the time.

## Good to know

- If Emkay asks for the microphone again after a restart, allow it once more. iOS sometimes asks
  again for apps on the Home Screen.
- An iPhone only listens while Emkay is on screen. That is exactly why Guided Access helps: Emkay
  never leaves the screen.
- Turn on **Do Not Disturb** or a Focus, so calls and notifications don't cover Emkay's face.
