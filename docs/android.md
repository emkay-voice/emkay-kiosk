# Emkay on an Android phone or tablet

A tablet on the wall or a spare phone on a stand by the bed: Android needs no extra apps for this.
Everything below is built into Android and Chrome.

Menu names differ a little between makers (Samsung, Google, Lenovo, …). If a setting isn't where
this guide says, search for it in the Settings app.

## 1. Install Emkay as an app

1. Open **Chrome** and go to <https://app.emkay.ai> (or your Emkay Home's address).
2. Open Chrome's **⋮** menu and choose **Install app** (on some versions: **Add to Home screen →
   Install**).
3. Start Emkay from its new icon. It now runs full screen as its own app.
4. Let Emkay finish its first start and allow the **microphone** when asked. Chrome remembers the
   choice.

## 2. Keep the screen on

- Say *"Hey Emkay, keep the screen on"* and *"Go full screen"*, or open the ⚙ menu → control panel →
  *Screen*.
- If the screen still goes dark, you can also turn on **Developer options → Stay awake** (the
  screen stays on while charging). To show Developer options, tap **Build number** seven times under
  **Settings → About phone**.

## 3. Pin Emkay to the screen

App pinning keeps one app on the screen until you unpin it.

1. Go to **Settings → Security & privacy → More security settings → App pinning** (Samsung:
   **Settings → Security and privacy → Other security settings → Pin windows**) and turn it on.
   Also turn on *Ask for PIN before unpinning*.
2. Open Emkay, open **Recents** (the overview of open apps), tap Emkay's icon at the top of its
   card and choose **Pin**.

To unpin: on gesture navigation, swipe up and hold; with buttons, hold **Back** and **Overview**
together. Then enter the PIN.

## 4. Power and battery

- Keep the device on its charger.
- Turn on the charging limit if there is one. Samsung: **Settings → Battery → Battery protection**.
  Pixel: **Settings → Battery → Charging optimisation**. Other makers often call it *battery
  protection* or *80 % limit*.
- Turn off battery optimisation for Chrome, so Android doesn't put Emkay to sleep: **Settings →
  Apps → Chrome → Battery → Unrestricted**.

## Good to know

- Turn on **Do Not Disturb**, so notifications don't cover Emkay's face. On a phone that is only
  used for Emkay, you can also take out the SIM card.
- Older devices with little memory get a lighter speech model. If it is still slow, let your
  [Emkay Home](https://github.com/emkay-voice/emkay-home) do the speech.
